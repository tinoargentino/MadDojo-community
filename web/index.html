Settings

Beat grid

Detected beats are a starting point. Adjust the grid if the song feels off.

Bar loops group beats using this meter. Automatic downbeat detection is not assumed.

Pitch lab

A positive alignment moves your pitch comparison earlier in the song. Headphones keep the backing out of your microphone. Pitch feedback does not assess breath support, tone, or vocal health.

Audio engine

Play a song to see the audio engine’s reported output latency.

Wired headphones or an audio interface give the most immediate response. Bluetooth adds its own delay.

Open in Ardour

  1. Download and unzip the session.

    Move the whole folder into Music on your Mac. Keep its audio files together.

  2. Open the .ardour file in Ardour.

    Use Ardour 9.8 or newer. Select CoreAudio and your microphone/headphone device.

  3. Loop a phrase.

    Space starts and stops. L plays the loop. The guide track has its own mute button and fader.

  4. Keep a take.

    Choose your input on Your input, arm that track, enable transport recording, and press Space. Save your session on the Mac.

The bundle also includes beat-based loop actions, vocal controls, and a short setup guide. Pitch lab remains available here as an experiment.
